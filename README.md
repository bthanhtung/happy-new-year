# 🎆 Happy New Year

Fireworks over the city, a countdown to midnight and a New Year wish for someone you love.

<img src="docs/preview.jpg" alt="Happy New Year 2027 in gold over a night city skyline with fireworks" width="760">

</div>

## What's inside

- **Countdown to midnight** in your own time zone; the last 10 seconds fill the screen, then the year flips and the finale goes off
- **Fireworks** over a city skyline: peony, ring, willow, crossette and heart shells — tap anywhere in the sky to launch one, or press **Launch fireworks**
- A rotating New Year wish; during the first week of January the page says *Welcome, 2027*
- **Send a card**: write who it's for, who it's from and a line from you, then copy the link or share it from your phone
- Works on phones; respects `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Links

| Add to the URL | Does |
|---|---|
| `?to=Mia&from=Tung&msg=Wishing%20you%20a%20brave%20new%20year` | A personal card: *Dear Mia,* above the title, the message and your name below |
| `?demo=10` | Pretends midnight is 10 seconds away, to see the countdown and the finale |

## Run it locally

Plain HTML, CSS and canvas — no build step, no images to load.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/bthanhtung/happy-new-year.git
cd happy-new-year
npx serve .        # or simply open index.html
```

To put it online, drop the folder on [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) or turn on GitHub Pages.
